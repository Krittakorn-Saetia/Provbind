# Figures and the numbers behind them

- **Final results (9 October 2026):** `data/final-2026-10-09/` holds the numbers. To draw the paper figures
  from them, follow `docs/FIGURES-HOWTO.md` (one command, no VM needed).
- **Older charts:** the PNG and SVG files in this folder are the comparison charts of 2 October, and
  `2026-10-01/` those of 1 October, drawn by `eval/plots.py` for `docs/ROLE1-COMPARISON-2026-10-01.md`.
  They use the original sensor policies and are not the paper figures.
- `data/comparison-2026-10-01.json` and `data/comparison-2026-10-02.json` are the comparison tables
  behind those older charts.
